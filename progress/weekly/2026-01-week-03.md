# Week 2 - January 2026
📅 January 11 - January 17, 2026

## Summary
The third week focused heavily on database modeling and restructuring to better
represent tournament data.

Initial database models were added early in the week, but issues with migrations
and relational constraints required further investigation. As understanding
improved, the database structure was revised to more accurately reflect
tournament relationships, including support for cyclical associations.

While progress was made, the increasing complexity of database design and the
cognitive load of balancing multiple BME-FON projects began to surface during
this week.


## What Worked
- Iterating on the database schema instead of forcing an early design
- Identifying limitations in the initial structure and adjusting accordingly
- Successfully resolving issues related to cyclical relationships
- Grounding schema decisions in real tournament representation needs

## What Didn't
- Database migrations did not cooperate initially, slowing early progress
- Reworking the schema required revisiting previous assumptions
- The cumulative complexity of database work and parallel project demands began
  to impact focus and available energy
